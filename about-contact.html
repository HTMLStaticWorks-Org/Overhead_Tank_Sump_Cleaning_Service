About AQUAGUARD

Dedicated to Clean Water Storage & Hygienic Living

AQUAGUARD is a specialised water-storage hygiene service focused on overhead water tanks and underground sumps. Rather than treating tank cleaning as a casual side-task of general plumbing, our operations are built specifically around clean dewatering, sediment extraction, interior surface care, and sanitization.

We serve independent homeowners, apartment Resident Welfare Associations (RWAs), commercial offices, clinics, educational campuses, and hospitality properties. Every business detail on this page is structured for straightforward customization by your service team.

Our Cleaning Approach

  • Valve isolation to protect building pipelines
  • Wet-vacuum extraction of bottom silt & mud
  • Material-appropriate wall scrubbing & jet wash

Equipment & Crew Hygiene

  • Dedicated sanitized gumboots, gloves & PPE
  • Submersible dewatering & vacuum sludge units
  • Fine-mist disinfection sprayers & work lighting
