# AQUAGUARD Website Pages

All primary HTML pages are located in the project root directory as specified in the project structure for clean relative routing across static web servers:

- [`index.html`](../index.html) — Primary Homepage (Clean water safety, service discovery, interactive before/after comparison)
- [`home-2.html`](../home-2.html) — Alternative Homepage (Professional cleaning process, hygiene standards & customer trust)
- [`services.html`](../services.html) — Services Page (6 detailed service categories, visual 6-step process, interactive FAQ accordion)
- [`pricing.html`](../pricing.html) — Pricing Page (Overhead Tank, Underground Sump, Combined, and Apartment/Commercial packages + transparent comparison)
- [`about.html`](../about.html) — About Us Page (Brand story, mission, equipment standards, crew hygiene protocols, and who we serve)
- [`contact.html`](../contact.html) — Contact Us & Booking Page (Business hours, phone/email, coverage areas, map visual, and 10-field validated Enquiry Form)
- [`dashboard.html`](../dashboard.html) — Customer Dashboard (Booking cleaning visits, viewing service reports, recurring bi-annual reminders, and invoice management)
- [`login.html`](../login.html) — Customer Sign In Page (Clean centered box, brand top-center, stacked single-column inputs, Google & Apple social sign-in)
- [`signup.html`](../signup.html) — Customer Sign Up Page (Clean centered box, brand top-center, stacked single-column inputs, password strength meter, Google & Apple social sign-up)
