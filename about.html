Why We Exist

Raising the Standard for Stored Water Hygiene

Most building maintenance plans focus heavily on visible plumbing fixtures while overlooking the very chambers where thousands of litres of domestic water sit undisturbed for months.

The Challenge

Sediment & Biofilm Buildup

Overhead tanks and underground sumps accumulate fine silt from municipal and borewell lines, forming dense floor mud and algae biofilms. Traditional manual cleaning often flushed this sludge directly into home taps or scratched food-grade tank linings.

  • Abrasive scrubbers damage plastic polymer linings
  • Dirty sump water contaminates freshly cleaned overhead tanks
  • Untrained personnel enter tanks in dirty street footwear
AQUAGUARD Standard The Solution

Structured Hygiene Protocol

We engineered a predictable 6-stage process using industrial submersible pumps, wet-vacuum sludge extractors, pressure washers, and food-grade mist disinfection.

  • Outlet valve isolation prevents sludge from reaching pipes
  • Synchronized cleaning of both underground sumps and overhead tanks
  • Sanitized dedicated boots, PPE suits, gloves & safety harnesses
Professional Gear

Our Equipment & Personnel Standards

We deploy specialized machinery specifically designed for water tank hygiene, avoiding improvised methods that risk property damage or contamination.

Sanitized PPE & Boots

Our cleaning crew wears clean waterproof jumpsuits, safety helmets, masks, and sanitized gumboots reserved strictly for clean water tank interiors.

Submersible Pumps

High-head, high-volume submersible pumps empty water quickly down to the floor silt layer, directing discharge cleanly to storm drains.

Wet-Vacuum Desilters

High-suction vacuum extractors remove dense clay, borehole silt, and rust flakes from tank corners and floor grooves without manual dragging.
